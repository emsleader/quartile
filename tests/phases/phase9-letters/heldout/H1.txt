Dear Authors,

Your paper "Imaginary Banks and Pretend Loans" has now been evaluated by two referees. Based on their advice, my decision is major revision.

Comments from the editor
Point 1. The paper reads like a report; please restructure it around one research question.
Point 2. Compare your estimates with earlier studies of pretend loans.

REVIEWER 1
1 - The authors should test whether the results hold for the sub-sample of small banks.
2 - It is unclear how missing values were treated.
3 - Provide the code used to build the panel.

REVIEWER 2
* The literature review omits work on relationship lending.
* The tables report too many decimals; use three.
* I recommend adding a discussion of managerial implications for loan officers.
