Owner experience and digital adoption in small firms

Abstract

We survey owners of small service firms with a questionnaire and Likert items.

1. Introduction

Small firms adopt digital tools at different speeds. We study why.

2. Methods

We sent a questionnaire to 240 firm owners. No ethics approval was required because no human participants were involved; the analysis uses published firm accounts only.

3. Results

Respondents with more experience report faster adoption.

4. Conclusion

Experience matters for adoption.
