Do Cash Buffers Shorten Site Opening Delays in Service Firms?

Abstract

We study the timing of site openings in service firms using a panel of firms.

1. Introduction

Small service firms often delay opening new sites because finance arrives late (Smith, 2019). About 40% of planned sites open more than a year late.

Jones (2020) and Lee et al. (2021) study credit access in manufacturing but do not examine service firms.

This paper examines whether cash buffers shorten the delay between signing and opening.

2. Data and methods

We use a panel of 300 firms observed over five years.

3. Results

Cash buffers are associated with shorter delays.
