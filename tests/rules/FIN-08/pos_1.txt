Access to Finance in Small Firms

Abstract

We describe credit constraints among small firms.

1. Introduction

Credit matters for small firms.

2. Data and Methods

We use the World Bank Enterprise Surveys for Hungary (2019), 1,021 firms. The questionnaire asks owners whether a loan application was rejected. Respondents answer on a five-point Likert scale.

3. Results

Thirty percent of firms report a rejected loan application.
