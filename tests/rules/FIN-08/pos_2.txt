Access to Finance in Small Firms

Abstract

We describe credit constraints among small firms.

1. Introduction

Credit matters for small firms.

2. Data and Methods

We use the WBES data for Hungary (2019), 1,021 firms. Estimates are unweighted and we do not apply the sampling weights. The questionnaire asks owners whether a loan application was rejected. Respondents answer on a five-point Likert scale.

3. Results

Thirty percent of firms report a rejected loan application.
