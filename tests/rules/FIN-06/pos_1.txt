Firm Leverage and Growth

Abstract

We study how leverage relates to sales growth in 1,240 Hungarian SMEs.

1. Introduction

Small firms borrow to grow. This paper estimates the link between debt and growth.

2. Data and Methods

We use annual accounts for 2015 to 2022 and estimate OLS regressions of sales growth on leverage with firm and year fixed effects.

Control variables include firm age and size.

3. Results

4. Conclusion

Leverage matters for growth in small firms.
