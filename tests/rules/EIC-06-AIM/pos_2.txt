Cash Buffers Shorten the Delay Between Signing and Opening in Service Firms

Abstract

We study the timing of site openings in service firms using a panel of firms.

1. Introduction

Small service firms often delay opening new sites because finance arrives late (Smith, 2019).

Jones (2020) and Lee et al. (2021) do not examine service firms.

This paper does not aim to settle the debate on credit rationing.

2. Data and methods

We use a panel of 300 firms observed over five years.

3. Results

Cash buffers are associated with shorter delays.
