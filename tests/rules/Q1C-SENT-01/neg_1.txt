# Abstract

We study how small service firms finance new equipment when bank credit is scarce. We use a survey of 3,104 firms in nine European countries from 2016 to 2021. Firms with a rejected loan application delay equipment replacement by about fourteen months. The delay is longest for young firms and for firms without collateral. Leasing and supplier credit only partly replace bank loans. The results point to simple changes in how lenders assess service firms.
