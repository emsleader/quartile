## Editorial-exemplar order for the target manuscript

- **Introduction and conceptual positioning:** A1 + A2, then B2 for historical institutionally specific motivation.
- **Data and firm survey eligibility:** A4 + A5; distinguish survey-response/sample composition from the underlying economic population.
- **Formal regression models and controls:** A1 + A3 + A6.
- **Cross-section vs matched-panel and decomposition:** A2 as a clear writing illustration, with independent survey-methods/Q1 papers for identification limitations.
- **Policy-facing discussion:** B1 (careful correlation vs causation), A1 and B2 for MNB-specific context.
- **Conclusion:** observe how A1/A3/B1 retain the principal estimand, subgroup evidence and scope conditions; do not imply effects of inflation, credit programmes or monetary policy that were not estimated.

## Integrity / source-access boundaries

FER is the **target journal**, not an additional set of automatically Q1 articles. Never classify the FER examples as SCImago Q1 solely because they were published in FER. Its official articles are marked CC BY-NC 4.0, which does not license copying passages into a new manuscript. The research engine may mine structural characteristics where legally permitted, but must not reproduce published authors' prose.

The FER examples do not resolve the target paper's methodological identification, attrition, wave weights or 2019 versus 2023 comparability by themselves. Those require the actual World Bank microdata and documentation, plus verified scholarly survey-methodological references.
