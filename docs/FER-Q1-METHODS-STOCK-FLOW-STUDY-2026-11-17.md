# FER manuscript: Q1 paper reading register, model types and reporting conventions

Scope (17 Nov 2026): 10 papers published in journals currently SCImago 2025 Q1, plus one directly relevant Q2 business-survey comparator. Full text or full author manuscript inspected for 6 Q1 studies; journal abstract/preview for 4 others. Do NOT claim full text read for the latter. The Q1 label means the JOURNAL ranking in 2025, not a ranking assigned to an article.

## Article-specific source audit

### Q1-01 — Productivity and firm exit during the COVID-19 crisis: cross-country evidence
**Bibliography:** Muzi et al. (2023), *Small Business Economics*, DOI 10.1007/s11187-022-00675-w. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published full HTML — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9415258/
**Study material & sample:** WBES survey+follow-up across 34 countries; 2019/2020 baseline, up to three follow-up rounds
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Binary confirmed/assumed firm exit; baseline fixed-asset purchase indicator used as covariate
**Econometric/statistical type:** Logit with controls, fixed effects, marginal effects, cluster-robust errors
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Confirmed versus assumed closure and covariate adjustments
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Closest WBES methodological comparator; missing survey interview does NOT prove firm exit

### Q1-02 — Short-term financial constraints and SMEs’ investment decision
**Bibliography:** Nicolas (2022), *Small Business Economics*, DOI 10.1007/s11187-021-00488-3. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published full HTML — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8096609/
**Study material & sample:** France credit-access survey + FIBEN 2012–16; 92,488 survey-quarter observations, 7,778 independent firms; 5,652 firms in investment panel
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Investment flow / lagged total asset stock; binary indicators of credit constraints
**Econometric/statistical type:** Heckman selection probit and dynamic system GMM; matching, quantile regressions
**Uncertainty/robustness:** J-test, AR2 serial correlation, alternative instrumental variables, matching
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Strong formal model/diagnostics comparator; NOT appropriate to copy system GMM with two WBES waves

### Q1-03 — ECB unconventional monetary policy and SME access to finance
**Bibliography:** Finnegan & Kapoor (2023), *Small Business Economics*, DOI 10.1007/s11187-023-00730-0. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Publisher open-access full HTML — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11187-023-00730-0
**Study material & sample:** ECB/EC SAFE, 11,319 SME survey observations, 2014–19, stressed euro-area countries
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Binary credit constraint outcome, not fixed asset purchasing
**Econometric/statistical type:** Probit with sample-selection adjustment; leverage/risk interaction, lagged monetary-policy exposure and controls
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Alternative constraints and monetary proxies, age/size subgroups and countries
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Model to results to scoped policy discussion; NO direct monetary shock inference for Hungary

### Q1-04 — Tightening monetary policy and investment dynamics in the European Monetary Union
**Bibliography:** Canofari et al. (2025), *Journal of Corporate Finance*, DOI 10.1016/j.jcorpfin.2025.102853. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published open-access PDF text — https://iris.univpm.it/bitstream/11566/346252/3/1-s2.0-S092911992500121X-main.pdf
**Study material & sample:** Amadeus non-listed-firm panel 2011–21, 130,313 firms across 19 euro-area economies
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Capital accumulation/investment response to monetary surprises
**Econometric/statistical type:** Dynamic local projections; firm FE, sector-year FE, leverage × shocks and country factors
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Heterogeneity by leverage, debt maturity and credit environment; macro-period robustness
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Best policy-identification contrast: not reproducible with 2019/2023 binary WBES alone

### Q1-05 — Dynamic Olley-Pakes productivity decomposition with entry and exit
**Bibliography:** Melitz & Polanec (2015), *RAND Journal of Economics*, DOI 10.1111/1756-2171.12088. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published author-hosted PDF text — https://scholar.harvard.edu/files/melitz/files/melitz_et_al-2015-the_rand_journal_of_economics.pdf
**Study material & sample:** Slovenian manufacturing 1995–2000: 3,867 firms in 1995, 4,580 in 2000, 2,677 surviving firms
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Aggregate productivity, weighted market shares; real firm entry/exit
**Econometric/statistical type:** Algebraic additive decomposition: survivors, true entrants and exiters; compare conventions
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Alternative productivity and weights, appendix formulas
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Borrow clarity of identities and limitations; sample leavers in Hungary are NOT economic exits

### Q1-06 — Accounting for Nonignorable Unit Nonresponse and Attrition in Panel Studies with Refreshment Samples
**Bibliography:** Schifeling et al. (2015), *Journal of Survey Statistics and Methodology*, DOI 10.1093/jssam/smv007. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Full author manuscript text (journal version not compared) — https://www2.stat.duke.edu/~jerry/Papers/jssam15.pdf
**Study material & sample:** Simulation + AP/Yahoo 2007–08 election panel with refreshment interviews
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Nonresponse and attrition probabilities; no corporate-investment dependent variable
**Econometric/statistical type:** Bayesian additive-nonignorable selection and joint-data model; sensitivity analysis
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Assumptions on baseline and refreshment unit nonresponse
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Demonstrates selection models need assumptions; IPW alone cannot remove MNAR selection

### Q1-07 — Do loan subsidies boost the real activity of small firms?
**Bibliography:** Horváth & Lang (2021), *Journal of Banking & Finance*, DOI 10.1016/j.jbankfin.2020.105988. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Publisher introduction, abstracts and section previews only — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426620302508
**Study material & sample:** Hungarian MNB subsidy programme (2013), linked bank-credit registry and corporate financial records
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Real investment, employment and firm efficiency—not binary WBES purchase participation
**Econometric/statistical type:** Propensity-score matching, counterfactual programme evaluation and simulation-based robustness (as described in publisher preview)
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Programme access heterogeneity and alternative simulation assessment
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Best local economic-policy comparison; its treatment effect is not transferable to our dataset

### Q1-08 — As the market churns: productivity and firm exit in developing countries
**Bibliography:** Aga & Francis (2017), *Small Business Economics*, DOI 10.1007/s11187-016-9817-7. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published abstract only; World Bank precursor available — https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/sbusec/v49y2017i2d10.1007_s11187-016-9817-7.html
**Study material & sample:** World Bank Enterprise Surveys panel in 47 economies
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Firm market exit vs unresolved operating status
**Econometric/statistical type:** Firm-exit predictors and uncertainty corrections (from abstract); exact published regression not yet extracted
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Alternative classification of firms with uncertain operating status
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Cite conservative true-closure definition; do not claim non-interviews are firm deaths

### Q1-09 — Nonignorable Attrition in Multi-Period Panels With Refreshment Samples
**Bibliography:** Hoonhout & Ridder (2019), *Journal of Business & Economic Statistics*, DOI 10.1080/07350015.2017.1345744. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published abstract only; older working paper available — https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/07350015.2017.1345744
**Study material & sample:** Longitudinal survey selection with refreshment sample, multiple periods
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Attrition/selection hazard; no investment measure
**Econometric/statistical type:** Sequential additive-nonignorable identification framework
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Sensitivity of identification to additional model assumptions
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Methodological foundation; refreshment does not nonparametrically solve all missingness

### Q1-10 — Financial constraints on investment: Effects of firm size and the financial crisis
**Bibliography:** Authors in DOI metadata (2019), *Research in International Business and Finance*, DOI 10.1016/j.ribaf.2018.09.006. **2025 Q1**.
**Source read:** Published abstract plus indexed article excerpts only — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ribaf.2018.09.006
**Study material & sample:** United Kingdom manufacturing survey around financial crisis
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Fixed-investment intentions, NOT realised asset purchases
**Econometric/statistical type:** Size-heterogeneous regressions; indexed excerpt discusses Hausman endogeneity test
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Exact model/sample/diagnostics remain unverified
**FER transfer / prohibition:** Strong outcome-type caution: planned vs actual vs binary purchase measures differ

### Q2-01 — Using Administrative Data to Evaluate Sampling Bias in a Business Panel Survey
**Bibliography:** D’Aurizio & Papadia (2019), *Journal of Official Statistics*, DOI 10.2478/jos-2019-0004. **2025 Q2 — excluded from strict Q1**.
**Source read:** Published full PDF text — https://reference-global.com/download/article/10.2478/jos-2019-0004.pdf
**Study material & sample:** Bank of Italy Invind panel ~4,000 firms per year 2002–13 matched to financial statements
**Outcome type / stock-flow distinction:** Firm performance indicators and sample entry/attrition
**Econometric/statistical type:** Administrative benchmarking of survey estimates and weight-vintage sensitivity
**Uncertainty/robustness:** Survivors/entrants/dropouts and differential size sensitivity
**FER transfer / prohibition:** BEST direct survey-composition comparator; journal Q2 in SCImago 2025, never call it Q1

## Verified writing-rhythm measurements from published full-text HTML

| Paper | Approx. introduction body paragraphs | Median words / intro paragraph | Approx. conclusion body paragraphs | Median words / conclusion paragraph |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Finnegan & Kapoor (2023) | 12 | 150 | 6 | 210 |
| Nicolas (2022) | 13 | 95 | 3 | 79 |
| Muzi et al. (2023) | 12 | 187 | 4 | 126 |

Metric method: text blocks separated by blank lines in rendered publisher/PMC HTML, excluding section headings, figure captions and tables, minimum 120 characters. Some footnotes may affect counts. These are **descriptive observations**, never compulsory paragraph targets.

Rhetorical sequence: first establish the economic problem; introduce conflicting mechanisms or estimands; give the dataset and credible identification boundary; put formal equations alongside operational variable definitions; report baseline estimate → uncertainty → competing explanations → robustness; finish with an implication scoped to the actual evidence. Keep the previous paragraph locked and draft one approved paragraph at a time.

## Critical lessons for the Hungarian study

1. WBES K.4 is fixed-asset purchase **incidence**. Do not equate it with capital-stock accumulation, monetary expenditure, investment intensity, intentions or the estimated effect of a policy shock.
2. The 15.5 percentage-point discrepancy is the difference between two estimands with different eligibility and weighting, not a direct measure of selection bias.
3. In the decomposition, survey sample turnover means firms observed in only one wave; it is not necessarily a firm birth or business closure. The distinction is supported by Muzi, Aga–Francis and D’Aurizio–Papadia.
4. The core additions to satisfy FER are exact equations for the weighted repeated cross-section, matched change, symmetric decomposition, weighted entrant–leaver LPM, re-interview logit, IPW and the joint bootstrap; plus diagnostics, not gratuitous GMM/local projections.
5. Monetary/energy/credit developments are policy context, not estimated causes. The reported FY2022 investment questions cannot capture a loan programme launched in 2023.
6. Re-interview IPW depends on observed baseline variables and assumes appropriate overlap/conditional ignorability. It does not solve nonignorable selection without additional assumptions; show propensity, effective n and covariate balance.
7. Use FER journal papers to calibrate submission house style separately; these ten Q1 benchmarks are for substantive method and article architecture, not copying prose.
